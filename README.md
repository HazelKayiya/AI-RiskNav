# AI-RiskNav
A digital governance dashboard for board oversight of AI and emerging technologies, that integrates Environmental, Social, and Economic (E–S–Ec) sustainability lenses to support responsible AI decision-making.

🔹 Purpose: To make AI governance more transparent and interpretable  
🔹 Focus: Digital Risk, Responsible AI, Board Oversight  
🔹 Keywords: AI Governance, Sustainability, Decision Support, Risk Management
